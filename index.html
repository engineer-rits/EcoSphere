YOUR ECO TO-DO LIST

Small actions.
Real difference.

Pick one thing to try today. You don't need to do everything to make a start.

01 / EVERYDAY♧
REDUCE WASTE

Bring a reusable bottle.

Keep a refillable bottle in your bag and choose refill stations where available.

02 / AFTER CLASS☼
SAVE ENERGY

Switch it off.

Turn off lights, fans and screens when a room is empty and it's safe to do so.

03 / EVERY MEAL♲
REDUCE WASTE

Sort your waste.

Check local bin labels and separate recyclable, wet and general waste correctly.

04 / WITH FRIENDS✿
HELP YOUR CAMPUS

Join a clean-up drive.

Take part in a campus clean-up or help organise one with your student group.

05 / ON THE MOVE↗
SAVE ENERGY

Walk short distances.

When practical and safe, walk or cycle for nearby trips instead of taking a ride.

06 / SHARE THE IDEA♡
HELP YOUR CAMPUS

Inspire one friend.

Share a simple sustainability tip and invite someone to try it with you.
